Static Risk Report→Living Investigation

A company doesn't stay the same. Why should its risk report?

Traditional risk assessment is a snapshot. Deepin maintains a living investigation. When a material event is detected, Deepin reopens the investigation, compares the new state with the company's history, collects the evidence and updates the risk context.

Evidence

Don't just show me the risk. Show me why.

deepin.risk is not a black-box score. Every change in risk context comes with its findings, its sources and the reasoning that connects them.

  1. 01

    Source

    Registry announcements, court records and external sources, each with its date.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    The facts that changed, kept with the company's history.

  3. 03

    Finding

    What is materially different since the last investigation.

  4. 04

    Reasoning

    Why the findings matter together, against your policies.

  5. 05

    Recommendation

    A suggested next step that your team approves or adjusts.

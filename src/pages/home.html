How Deepin works

From signal to approved action.

Deepin follows the same path your analysts already take. It does the searching and comparing, and it stops for a person before anything changes.

  1. Observe

    Watch registries, transactions, logs and documents.

  2. Investigate

    Search across systems and gather what an analyst would.

  3. Reason

    Compare and link findings, check them against policy.

  4. Propose

    A recommendation with its sources and reasoning.

  5. Human approval

    Your team approves, edits or rejects.

  6. Act

    The decision goes back to your systems, fully audited.

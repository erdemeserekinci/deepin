Evidence

Evidence before decision.

Deepin does not hand you an AI answer. It hands you an investigation: every reason, every source, and a person who signs off.

  1. 01

    Source

    Each finding links to the record, document or system it came from.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    The facts are collected, dated and kept with the case.

  3. 03

    Reasoning

    The logic from evidence to conclusion is written out, with the policy it applies.

  4. 04

    Recommendation

    A proposed decision, never a silent one.

  5. 05

    Human approval

    A person approves, edits or rejects before anything changes.

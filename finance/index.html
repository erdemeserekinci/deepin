The problem

Every dispute is a small investigation.

A single chargeback can touch the card system, core banking, the acquirer, the merchant and a pile of documents. Multiply that by thousands of cases a month.

High volume

Cases arrive every day and the queue rarely empties.

Hard deadlines

Scheme timeframes leave little room for slow evidence collection.

Scattered data

Transaction, authentication, merchant and document data live in different systems.

Real losses

A missed deadline or a wrong call becomes a write-off, a fee or a complaint.

How it works

The case path, end to end.

deepin.finance follows the steps your dispute analysts already take, and stops for a person before any action is taken.

  1. Transaction

    A dispute is raised on a card transaction.

  2. Case data

    Authorization, clearing and cardholder data are pulled in.

  3. Evidence

    Authentication logs, merchant responses and documents are collected.

  4. Rules & policies

    Scheme rules and your internal policies are applied.

  5. Investigation

    Findings are weighed and a decision is proposed with its reasoning.

  6. Human approval

    Your analyst approves, edits or rejects the proposal.

  7. Action

    The decision goes back to your systems with a full audit trail.

Merchant context

Look at the transaction and the company behind it.

Many financial cases need both. While a merchant's transaction is being investigated, deepin.finance can check the merchant company itself using deepin.risk, in the same case.
